I am a third-year PhD student in Economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My primary research interests are in Industrial Organization and Applied Microeconomics, with a particular focus on competition, market power, and market design.

My research spans several markets, including electricity, healthcare, public procurement, and retail gasoline. I am particularly interested in understanding how market structure, asymmetric information, and vertical relationships shape firms' behavior and competitive outcomes. My work examines issues related to auctions, bargaining, mergers, and collusion, combining theoretical and empirical approaches to study how competition operates in different institutional settings.
