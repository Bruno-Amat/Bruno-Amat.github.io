# Working Paper

## When Wholesalers Join the Cartel: Vertical Coordination in Gasoline Markets

*Submitted*

Abstract: This paper studies how upstream participation changes cartel organization and outcomes in Brazilian gasoline markets. Using antitrust case records, I document that vertical cartels involve wholesalers in coordinating retailers and disciplining deviations through wholesale terms, unlike retail cartels relying on downstream coordination. I combine eight cartel cases with station-level price data and a matching difference-in-differences design, finding larger retail-price and retail-markup effects in vertical-cartel cases, while small retailers capture smaller markup gains and face higher wholesale markups. A model shows how discriminatory wholesale terms weaken retailers’ incentives to remain outside the cartel, broaden participation, and support higher retail prices.
